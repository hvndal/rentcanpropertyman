How it works

Set up once. Then let it run.

From adding your first unit to getting paid, RentCan keeps every step in one place — no spreadsheets, no chasing.

Step 01

Add your property

List your units, set the rent, and add tenant details in minutes. Upload agreements and documents so everything lives in one place.

Step 02

Tenants pay in-app

Tenants pay rent through Razorpay — UPI, cards, or netbanking. You get paid and notified instantly, with automatic reminders before each due date.

Maintenance handled

Tenants raise a request with photos. You approve the cost from your phone, a vendor is dispatched, and you see it through to done.

Good to know

Questions, answered.

Everything you might wonder before you download.

Do I have to be in India to use RentCan?
Not at all. Manage your property from anywhere in the world — your tenants use the app locally in the Tricity, and you stay in control from your phone.
How do tenants pay rent?
Through the app via Razorpay — UPI, cards, or netbanking. You're notified the moment rent is paid, and reminders go out automatically before each due date.
What if a tenant pays late?
RentCan sends automatic reminders ahead of the due date, and your dashboard shows payment status at a glance so nothing slips through the cracks.
Is there a setup fee or lock-in?
No setup fee and no lock-in — cancel anytime. Your first month is free, and you don't need a card to start.
Can I manage more than one property?
Yes — one dashboard for all of them. Your plan covers 1–2 properties; each extra one is ₹1,000/month, and any vacant unit drops to ₹499/month.
Is RentCan a broker?
No. RentCan helps with listing, coordination and paperwork, but we're not a real estate broker, agent or property manager unless agreed in writing — and you always make the final tenant decision. Full terms live in our Service Agreement.
