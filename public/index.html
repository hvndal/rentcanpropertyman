Flagship Service

Monthly Physical Property Inspection

Every managed property receives a scheduled on-site inspection by a RentCan field representative. Instead of relying solely on tenant updates, we physically visit your property to document its condition and identify issues before they become costly problems.

This is why landlords pay RentCan every month — because we actively look after the property in the real world, not just through software.

Every inspection includes

  • • High-resolution photographs
  • • Detailed digital report
  • • Property condition summary
  • • Priority issue recommendations
  • • Historical inspection records
